Counting methodology, archive sources, and included entries

This map is based on 297 independently maintained experiment or project-series dossiers. Historical experiments, operating facilities, prototypes, named proposals and multipurpose observatories with a documented dark-matter programme are included. This is a catalog, not a census of every experiment worldwide or a count of simultaneously operating detectors.

Click an experiment name on the right to read its scientific overview, detector concept, key parameters, programme, results and references, and to view its representative image. Photographs, historical prototypes and future design concepts retain their original stage descriptions.

Counts follow the detector or explicitly identified candidate site, rather than collaboration nationality or headquarters. A series is counted once in each associated country; therefore country totals may sum to more than the unique global total. Space missions, global networks and projects without a confirmed site are listed separately. Europe is the deduplicated union of the country set specified in the catalog; Russia and Greenland retain separate counts.

Statuses refer to the evidence dates stated in each dossier. An undated website is not proof of operation on the catalog update date. The future filter includes proposed, planned, research-and-development, construction and commissioning entries; series containing several stages remain in their own mixed-status category.

The map uses the same geographic paths, projection, typography, colors and page layout as the supplied Neutrino Experiments World Map. Detailed experiment text and images are read from separate files when a dossier is opened. Image captions contain numbered citations; scientific references and image credits are collected at the end of each dossier.
